How scheduling works

Each question is scheduled with SM-2, the SuperMemo spaced-repetition algorithm. It tracks how easy the question is for you and how long to wait before asking again.

  • A new question comes back the next day.
  • Each correct answer stretches the gap (1 day, then 3, then about a week, growing each time), so you spend less time on what you know.
  • A wrong answer counts as a lapse: the question starts over and comes back sooner.
